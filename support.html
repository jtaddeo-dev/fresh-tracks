Support

Fresh Tracks is a downhill time trial for iPhone and iPad: skis or a snowboard, tilt to steer, down handcrafted runs on five mountains. Need a hand? We are happy to help.

Email support

Write to jtaddeohpu@gmail.com and we will reply within a few business days. Telling us your device model and iOS version helps.

How do I steer?

Hold your phone however is comfortable and tap to drop in: that hold becomes straight ahead. Tilt left and right to carve. Hold a finger anywhere to tuck for speed, and swipe up to jump. Prefer not to tilt? Turn on Touch steer in Settings and drag a thumb left and right instead.

Steering feels too twitchy or too slow

Open Settings (the gear on the title screen) and move Tilt sensitivity. If straight ahead drifts, pause and tap Resume: it recalibrates to how you are holding the phone.

What happens when I crash?

You are back at the last gate you passed, already moving. The clock keeps running, so every crash costs time.

How do I unlock more trails?

Each mountain's trails open one after another as you finish the one before. Bluebird Peak is free.

What does the All-Mountain Pass unlock?

The Pass is a one-time purchase that unlocks every mountain after Bluebird Peak, including mountains added in future updates. Bluebird Peak and its trails are free.

I bought the Pass. How do I restore it?

Swipe to any locked mountain, tap to unlock it, then tap Restore purchase. Purchases restore free to any device signed in with the same Apple Account, and the Pass works with Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset best times. This clears your times and medals on this device.

Do you collect my data?

No. Fresh Tracks plays offline and collects no personal information. See the Privacy Policy.
