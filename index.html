Support

Fresh Tracks is a downhill time trial for iPhone and iPad: skis or a snowboard, down 50 handcrafted runs on five mountains. Need a hand? We are happy to help.

Email support

Write to jtaddeohpu@gmail.com and we will reply within a few business days. Telling us your device model and iOS version helps.

How do I steer?

Tap to drop in. Hold the left or right side of the screen to carve that way; a quick tap is a small correction. Hold both sides to tuck for speed, and swipe up to jump. In the air, swipe again for a flip or a spin.

Can I steer by tilting the phone?

No. Fresh Tracks steers by touch only, because holding the screen is far more precise than tilting.

What happens when I crash?

You are back at the last gate you passed, already moving. The clock keeps running, so every crash costs time.

How do I unlock more trails?

Every mountain is open. On each one, the first five trails are free and open one after another as you finish the one before.

What does the Trail Pass unlock?

The Trail Pass is a one-time purchase that unlocks the extra trails on every mountain (five on each, 25 in all), including trails added in future updates. The first five trails on every mountain are free.

I bought the Trail Pass. How do I restore it?

Purchases restore free to any device signed in with the same Apple Account, and the Trail Pass works with Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset best times. This clears your times and medals on this device.

Do you collect my data?

No. Fresh Tracks plays offline and collects no personal information. See the Privacy Policy.
