Fresh Tracks

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version: Fresh Tracks collects nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Who we are

Fresh Tracks ("the app") is made by JTLabs LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your progress on your device using standard iOS storage: best times, splits and medals per trail, which trails you have unlocked, your ride and outfit, and your settings. This data stays on your device. It is never sent to us, and it is removed when you delete the app or tap Reset best times in Settings.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your finishing times to its leaderboards. Game Center is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; we never receive your Apple Account information.

In-app purchase

Fresh Tracks offers one optional one-time purchase, the Trail Pass, which unlocks the extra trails on every mountain. The first five trails on every mountain are free without it. Purchases and restores are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party tracking code. It makes no network connections to our servers. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Fresh Tracks is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
